## 6. FAQ (5 Q&As, 30–50 words each, no invented numbers)

**Q1: What is a commercial track light?**
A: A commercial track light is a modular lighting system mounted on a continuous rail, allowing adjustable spotlight heads to illuminate specific areas. It provides flexible accent and general illumination for retail stores, galleries, and showrooms without requiring extensive ceiling rewiring.

**Q2: How do traditional and magnetic track systems differ?**
A: Traditional track uses line-voltage rails with mechanical connectors for robust, industrial-style installations. Magnetic track uses low-voltage rails with magnetic adapters, enabling tool-free module swaps and a sleeker aesthetic ideal for modern boutiques and architectural spaces.

**Q3: What makes good retail track lighting?**
A: Strong retail track lighting pairs high-CRI COB spotlights for accurate merchandise rendering with flexible beam angles. Magnetic or traditional systems both work when heads can be repositioned easily for seasonal displays and changing visual merchandising layouts.

**Q4: What should an RFQ include for a track light system supplier?**
A: Specify track type, voltage, finishes, rail lengths, and luminaire quantities. Include target CRI, CCT, beam angles, and dimming protocols, and request CAD/photometric files plus compliance documentation such as UL or IEC references for project compatibility [cite: UL] [cite: IEC].

**Q5: Can commercial track light systems mix with linear or recessed fixtures?**
A: Yes. Track usually carries adjustable accent layers while linear runs and recessed downlights handle ambient wash. Keep CCT and CRI consistent across layers, and approve samples for each fixture family before bulk production.
