## 6. FAQ (5 Q&As, 30–50 words each, no invented numbers)

**Q1: What does an LED flood light manufacturer produce?**
A: An LED flood light manufacturer builds directional high-output fixtures for sports fields, yards, facades, and industrial sites. The factory controls the housing and thermal path, optics, driver choice, sealing, and mounting hardware — not just the LED board inside the fixture.

**Q2: Which beam angle should I specify for LED flood lights?**
A: Match the beam to the task: narrow optics for throw and accenting, wide optics for even area coverage, and asymmetric optics where spill must be limited. Confirm the choice with the model's IES photometric file and a sample before ordering [cite: IES].

**Q3: What IP rating do outdoor LED flood lights need?**
A: It depends on exposure. Exposed area and sports fixtures need a rating suited to wet locations, and fixtures near standing water or washdown need more. Check the IP rating against IEC 60529 and the actual site conditions [cite: IEC].

**Q4: Which compliance documents should I ask a flood light manufacturer for?**
A: Ask for safety reports against IEC 60598 or UL/ETL listings for your market, IEC 60529 IP verification, CE where applicable, DLC listing status if you sell into US utility programs, and LM-79 plus LM-80/TM-21 data for the model [cite: IES] [cite: DLC].

**Q5: How do I vet an LED flood light manufacturer before ordering?**
A: Check category fit across flood light types, request photometric files and reliability data, run a structured sample program, confirm batch-to-batch CCT and finish consistency, review IQC/IPQC/OQC documentation, and confirm OEM or ODM support if you need customized fixtures.
