## 6. FAQ (5 Q&As, 30–50 words each, no invented numbers)

**Q1: What is an LED lighting manufacturer?**
A: An LED lighting manufacturer designs, builds, and tests fixtures in its own factory instead of only brokering finished goods. Buyers can usually verify production lines, quality gates, and sample traceability with the factory of record, then request OEM or ODM support when a catalog product is not enough.

**Q2: How is an LED lighting manufacturer different from a lighting sourcing agent?**
A: A manufacturer can show the production line, tooling, and test equipment behind a sample. A lighting sourcing agent helps buyers find, audit, and coordinate factories, but the agent is not the factory of record and cannot replace a direct factory capability check.

**Q3: What should buyers check before choosing an LED lighting manufacturer in China?**
A: Confirm the factory can make your product family, then review IQC, in-process, and OQC practices, test equipment, and destination-market safety files. Ask for IES photometric files, sample traceability, and a clear OEM or ODM path before you treat a quotation as factory-direct [cite: IES].

**Q4: What belongs in an RFQ for an LED lighting manufacturer?**
A: Specify the product family, approximate quantities, target CCT, CRI, beam, and dimming, plus IP or IK needs, finishes, packaging, labeling, inspection, Incoterms, and a sample plan. List the safety and efficiency documents required for the destination market, such as UL or IEC luminaire files [cite: UL] [cite: IEC].

**Q5: When should a buyer choose OEM vs ODM with a manufacturer?**
A: Use a catalog or private-label path when a standard fixture already fits. Choose OEM when you bring a defined design for the factory to produce. Choose ODM when you want the manufacturer to develop or adapt the design, and confirm tooling ownership before production.
