## 6. FAQ (5 Q&As, 30–50 words each, no invented numbers)

**Q1: What is the main difference between OEM and ODM in LED lighting?**
A: In OEM, the buyer provides the design and specifications, and the factory manufactures it. In ODM, the factory provides an existing design, and the buyer brands it. OEM offers high customization, while ODM offers faster time-to-market and lower upfront costs.

**Q2: Who owns the tooling and molds in an OEM project?**
A: In a properly structured OEM agreement, the buyer who pays for the custom tooling retains full ownership of the molds and dies. This must be explicitly stated in the manufacturing contract to prevent the factory from using your custom molds for other clients.

**Q3: What is a golden sample in LED manufacturing?**
A: A golden sample is the final, fully approved prototype that serves as the absolute baseline for mass production. Both the buyer and the manufacturer sign and date this sample. It dictates the exact cosmetic, mechanical, and photometric standards for the entire production run.

**Q4: Which certifications are essential when importing commercial LED lights?**
A: Essential certifications include IEC 60598 or UL 1598 for luminaire safety, IEC 61347 for driver safety, and IEC 62471 for photobiological safety. For market access, CE and RoHS are required for Europe, while DLC or ENERGY STAR may be needed for North American rebates.

**Q5: How does private label lighting differ from standard ODM?**
A: Private label lighting is a subset of ODM where the buyer selects a manufacturer's existing product and applies their own brand name, logo, and packaging. Standard ODM might involve minor customizations to the ODM design, whereas private label is strictly about branding an unmodified catalog product.
