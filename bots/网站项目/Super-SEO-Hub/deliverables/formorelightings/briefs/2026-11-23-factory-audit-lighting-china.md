### H2: Which types of lighting factory audits should buyers use?

Link [incoming quality control], [in-process quality control], and [outgoing quality control] rather than explaining those gates in full.
- Social / labor compliance audit when the buyer or brand program requires it: treat [amfori BSCI](https://www.amfori.org/amfori-bsci/), [Sedex SMETA](https://www.sedex.com/solutions/smeta-audit/), and [SA8000](https://sa-intl.org/programs/sa8000/) as programs a buyer may request. ### H2: What should you review before visiting the factory?
- Legal entity versus production address: record the company on the quotation, the factory of record, and the site you will walk. Ask for a clear explanation if they differ.
- Certificate pack, with limits: [ISO 9001](https://www.iso.org/standards/popular/iso-9000-family) is a quality-management standard, not a product safety approval and not a substitute for an on-site audit. Buyers should check issuer, scope, site, and validity, and may confirm whether the certification is issued in an [IAF](https://iaf.nu/en/home/) accredited system. If you mention the 2015 edition page, use [ISO 9001:2015 (ISO 62085)](https://www.iso.org/standard/62085.html) and tell readers to verify the edition stated on the actual certificate; do not invent a newer edition number.
- Product / market files for the destination: luminaire safety evidence such as [IEC 60598-1](https://webstore.iec.ch/en/publication/66620), tied to the exact SKU — not a generic catalog certificate. Link the manufacturer guide's certification section instead of copying its full list.
- Process map: which steps (SMT, assembly, aging, photometric test, packing) are in-house at the visited site, and which are subcontracted.
- **Place inline image 1 here:** `factory-audit-lighting-china-document-pack.webp`.

### H2: What should an on-site lighting factory audit check?

- Test capability that matches the fixtures: electrical safety tests, photometric or integrating-sphere / goniophotometer evidence when relevant, and sample traceability back to materials or work orders.
- Subcontracting boundaries: what is done off-site, and how the factory of record stays accountable.
- Use *factory audit lighting china* naturally 2–3 times across this H2 and the report H2 combined; do not stuff.
- One-sentence adjacency: OEM or ODM tooling and IP belong on the [OEM vs ODM lighting guide] — do not rewrite it.

### H2: What belongs in a useful factory audit report?

- A "pass" stamp without site, scope, and sampled evidence is not enough.
- Remote or video reviews: useful for screening and record checks, not equivalent to an on-site walk of the production and test areas. State this limit clearly; do not claim equivalence.

### H2: How should audit findings shape the first order?

## 6. FAQ (5 Q&As, 30–50 words each, no invented numbers)

**Q1: What is a factory audit for lighting in China?**
A: A factory audit for lighting in China is a structured review of the production site, quality process, and records behind a supplier — not a showroom tour or a certificate check. Buyers use it to confirm the legal entity, production address, process capability, and quality evidence before a first order.
*(Word count: 49.)*

**Q2: Does an ISO 9001 certificate replace a factory audit?**
A: No. An ISO 9001 certificate shows a quality-management system was certified for a stated scope and site at a point in time. Buyers should still verify the issuer, scope, site, and validity, then check whether the production line for their product family actually runs at that site.
*(Word count: 48.)*

**Q3: What should a lighting factory audit report include?**
A: A useful report names the legal entity and production address, audit date and scope, product family reviewed, and processes seen in-house versus subcontracted. It should reference the documents sampled, record gaps, and list follow-up actions before the first order — not just a pass or fail stamp.
*(Word count: 48.)*

**Q4: Can a remote or video review replace an on-site factory audit?**
A: A remote or video review can help screen a supplier and check records, but it is not equivalent to walking the production and test areas for the product family you will buy. Use it as a supplement, then confirm critical processes, equipment, and traceability on site before a first order.
*(Word count: 50.)*

**Q5: When should B2B buyers audit a lighting factory in China?**
A: Audit before a first production order, and again when the product family, production site, or key process changes. A factory audit is especially useful when the quotation looks factory-direct, when an agent is involved, or when destination-market safety files need to be tied to the actual manufacturing site.
*(Word count: 50.)*
