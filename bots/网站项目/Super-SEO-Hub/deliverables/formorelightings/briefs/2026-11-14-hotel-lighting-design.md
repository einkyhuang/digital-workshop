## 6. FAQ (5 Q&As, 30–50 words each, no invented numbers)

**Q1. What is hotel lighting design?**
A: Hotel lighting design is a layered specification process that balances guest comfort, brand identity, and operational efficiency. It defines ambient, accent, and task lighting across distinct zones, ties each layer to strict color quality metrics, and establishes fixture schedules for consistent multi-property rollouts.

**Q2. Which standards apply to hospitality lighting?**
A: Consult EN 12464-1 for back-of-house and front desk areas, the IES Lighting Handbook for hospitality guidance, and CIE 224:2017 for color fidelity metrics. ENERGY STAR and DLC serve as general efficiency references. Always confirm local adoption with your project authority.

**Q3. How does color temperature affect guest rooms?**
A: Warmer color temperatures like 2700K or 3000K create a relaxing, residential feel ideal for guest rooms, while 4000K might feel too clinical. For detailed comparisons on choosing the right warmth for different zones, consult specific color temperature guides before finalizing your specifications.

**Q4. What fixtures are used in hotel lobbies?**
A: Hotel lobbies typically use layered lighting, combining recessed or trimless downlights for general ambient light, magnetic track systems for flexible art accenting, and decorative pendants or LED strip coves for architectural interest. This combination ensures visual comfort while highlighting the brand identity.

**Q5. What must a hotel lighting RFQ include?**
A: A comprehensive RFQ must include floor plans, zone types, qualitative visual targets, CCT and CRI requirements, glare limits, and control protocols. It should also mandate IES photometric files for every SKU, strict LED binning consistency, and a physical sample review process.
