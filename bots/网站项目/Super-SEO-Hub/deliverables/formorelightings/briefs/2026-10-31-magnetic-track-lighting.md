## 6. FAQ (5 Q&As, 30–50 words each, no invented numbers)

**Q1: What voltage does magnetic track lighting use?**
A: Most systems are low-voltage DC, commonly 48V, operating as a SELV architecture. Voltage is not universal across suppliers, so confirm the rated system voltage, driver output, and SELV classification on the datasheet for the exact system you are specifying.

**Q2: Is magnetic track lighting compatible across brands?**
A: Not reliably. Magnetic attachment, contact geometry, and module profiles differ between suppliers, so modules and tracks from different brands may not mate or may not be electrically safe together. Specify track, driver, and modules as one matched system from a single supplier.

**Q3: Can magnetic track modules be repositioned after installation?**
A: Yes — that is a core benefit. Modules snap onto the track magnetically and can be moved or swapped by hand without tools, which suits retail displays and hospitality spaces where layout changes over time. Confirm the module retention force with your supplier.

**Q4: What driver and dimming details should I confirm?**
A: Confirm total connected module load against driver capacity with headroom, check voltage drop on long track runs, and match the dimming protocol — DALI, 0-10V, Triac, or Zigbee — between driver and control system. Ask for driver test reports to IEC 61347.

**Q5: What should I check on the module datasheet?**
A: Verify CRI and R9, beam angle, UGR where glare matters, photobiological safety to IEC 62471, and luminaire safety to IEC 60598 (and UL 1574 for North America). Request photometric files for the exact SKU, not the product family.
