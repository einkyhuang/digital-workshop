### H2: Which specs decide LED panel light performance?
- Uniformity and optical stack: backlit versus edge-lit constructions change how even the emitting face looks; ask for a lit sample, not just a catalog photo.
- Glare / UGR: indoor panels are often specified against a workplace glare target; link [UGR in office lighting] and use *ugr office lighting* or *led ugr* naturally once. Do not invent a numeric UGR rating for any Formore product.
- CCT: keep correlated color temperature consistent across a ceiling and adjacent fixtures; link [color temperature guide] and [3000K vs 4000K LED].
- CRI: matters where finishes, skin tones, and merchandising are judged; use *cri 90 led* once, naturally, and link [what is CRI in lighting].
- Driver and dimming: 0–10 V, DALI, or other protocols must be stated on the RFQ; link [LED dimming guide].
- Installation: recessed, surface, or suspended mounting, driver location, and service access — these belong in the drawing package, not as afterthoughts.
- No invented lux, wattage, efficacy, lifespan, or UGR figures; where a number is needed, use `[cite source]`. Workplace criteria can be framed against ISO/CIE 8995-1 (see Section 4) without quoting unofficial lux tables.

## 6. FAQ (5 Q&As, 30–50 words each, no invented numbers)

**Q1: What does an LED panel light manufacturer produce?**
A: An LED panel light manufacturer builds slim indoor ceiling luminaires that deliver even general lighting for offices, classrooms, and similar commercial interiors. The factory controls the optical stack, frame, driver, dimming interface, finish, and mounting hardware — not just the LED board inside the panel.

**Q2: How do LED panel lights differ from linear lights or downlights?**
A: Panels are typically used for wide, even ceiling illumination. Linear fixtures suit continuous runs and open-plan layouts, and downlights handle accents or circulation. Many projects combine them; choose the mix from the ceiling type and visual-comfort target, not from a single catalog page.

**Q3: Why does UGR matter when specifying LED panel lights?**
A: UGR describes how glaring a lighting installation may feel in an indoor workplace. For offices and similar rooms, buyers should set a glare method and review a lit sample plus the model's photometric file, then check the result against the project's workplace criteria.

**Q4: Which compliance documents should I ask a panel light manufacturer for?**
A: Ask for safety reports against IEC 60598 or UL/ETL listings for your market, photometric files (LM-79-style) and matching source-maintenance data, DLC listing status if you sell into US utility programs, and a written rule for substitutions. Verify each document against the exact SKU.

**Q5: How do I vet an LED panel light manufacturer before ordering?**
A: Check category fit across panel types, request photometric files, run a structured sample program for uniformity, CCT/CRI, dimming, and finish, confirm batch consistency, review IQC/IPQC/OQC documentation, and confirm OEM or ODM support if you need customized frames, sizes, or controls.
