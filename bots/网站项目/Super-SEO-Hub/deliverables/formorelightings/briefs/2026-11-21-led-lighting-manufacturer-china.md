## 6. FAQ (5 Q&As, 30–50 words each, no invented numbers)

**Q1: What is an LED lighting manufacturer in China?**
A: An LED lighting manufacturer in China is a factory that designs, tools, assembles, tests, and packs luminaires for commercial and export orders. It should be able to show a real production site, process control, and test or third-party reports — not only a catalog and a trading invoice.

**Q2: How do I tell a China lighting factory from a trading company?**
A: Ask for the legal entity and factory address, then confirm who owns the production process, who can change drawings, and whose name appears on test reports, invoices, and packing lists. A trader can be useful, but you still need to know and be able to audit the plant that will build the goods.

**Q3: Which documents should I request from a China LED lighting manufacturer?**
A: Request destination-market safety evidence (for example IEC 60598 or UL/ETL files for the exact model), photometric files, quality-system certificates whose scope matches the factory site, and a written substitution rule. Treat carton marks alone as insufficient; verify each document against the SKU you will order.

**Q4: Do I still need a factory audit if the manufacturer has ISO 9001?**
A: A current ISO 9001 certificate is useful evidence, but only if the scope, site, and expiry match the plant you will use. A factory audit in China — or a structured video walkthrough plus sample and record review — still helps you confirm that the certified site is the one producing your order.

**Q5: What should an RFQ to a China lighting manufacturer include?**
A: State the destination market and required files, product families and specs, drawing and labeling control, sample and photometric requests, inspection plan, packing, and Incoterms with a named place. Also state who is the manufacturer of record so test reports and shipping documents stay consistent.
