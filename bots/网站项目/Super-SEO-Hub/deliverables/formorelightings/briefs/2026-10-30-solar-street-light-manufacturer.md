## 6. FAQ (5 Q&As, 30–50 words each, no invented numbers)

**Q1: What should I check first when vetting a solar street light manufacturer?**
A: Start with factory capability and test reports. Confirm they can show CE and RoHS documentation, IEC 62133 battery safety reports, and IEC 61215 panel certificates — then request a sample and an aging-test record before committing to a production order.

**Q2: Is an all-in-one or split solar street light better for my project?**
A: All-in-one units install faster and reduce pole load, which suits retrofit and space-limited roads. Split systems handle higher-output loads and harsh climates better. Choose based on road class, sun hours, and maintenance access — not price alone.

**Q3: What battery chemistry should I specify?**
A: LiFePO4 is the common choice for solar street lighting because of its cycle life and thermal stability. Ask for the battery's IEC 62133 report and confirm the Wh rating at the system voltage you're specifying.

**Q4: What MOQ and lead time should I expect for OEM orders?**
A: MOQ and lead time vary by SKU, housing tooling, and customization level. Ask your supplier to quote MOQ per model, sample lead time, and production lead time in writing — and confirm whether tooling is owned by you or the factory.

**Q5: How do I verify lumen and wattage claims?**
A: Request third-party photometric and electrical test reports for the exact model, not the product family. Compare lm/W at the system level (including driver losses) and confirm test conditions match your operating environment.
