## 6. FAQ (5 Q&As, 30–50 words each, no invented numbers)

**Q1: What is the difference between commercial and residential LED lighting?**
A: Commercial LED lighting is designed for high-usage, large-scale environments requiring higher durability, stricter safety certifications, and advanced photometric performance compared to residential fixtures. It prioritizes lifecycle cost, energy efficiency, and specific light distributions for tasks like office work or warehouse operations.

**Q2: Why is UGR important for commercial office lighting?**
A: UGR (Unified Glare Rating) measures visual discomfort caused by glare. Office projects usually specify a maximum UGR value in the lighting design brief [cite source], so buyers should confirm each fixture's UGR data. Specifying low-UGR fixtures ensures a comfortable visual environment for employees working at computers.

**Q3: What does L70 mean in LED lighting lifespan?**
A: L70 indicates the time it takes for an LED's light output to depreciate to 70% of its initial lumens. It is the industry standard for defining the useful life of a commercial LED fixture, ensuring long-term performance and predictable maintenance schedules.

**Q4: Which certifications are required for commercial LED lighting in North America?**
A: For North America, commercial LED lighting typically requires UL 1598 for safety and DLC or ENERGY STAR listing for energy efficiency and rebate eligibility. Depending on the installation, FCC compliance for electronic controls and local building code energy standards may also apply.

**Q5: How do I choose the right beam angle for retail lighting?**
A: For retail track lighting, a narrow beam angle creates dramatic accent lighting to highlight specific merchandise. For general aisle illumination, a wider beam angle provides even, uniform light distribution across the floor space.
