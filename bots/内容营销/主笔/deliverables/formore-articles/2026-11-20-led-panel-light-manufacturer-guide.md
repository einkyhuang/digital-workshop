An LED panel light manufacturer builds slim, even-output ceiling luminaires for offices, classrooms, and other indoor commercial spaces, where uniformity, glare control, and consistent CCT/CRI decide whether a ceiling grid feels comfortable. Buyers should define the mounting type and visual-comfort target first, then lock UGR, CCT, CRI, driver and dimming, and destination-market safety evidence before requesting samples and photometric files.

![Modern open-plan office ceiling grid of square LED panel lights providing even, low-glare illumination, with no people or text visible.](/insights%20image/insights%20feature%20images/led-panel-light-manufacturer-office-grid.webp)

## What does an LED panel light manufacturer do?

An LED panel light manufacturer designs and builds slim indoor LED panel luminaires for even general lighting in offices, classrooms, and similar commercial interiors. It is not a design-only brand, and not simply a general lighting factory with one catalog panel.

The blocks an LED panel light manufacturer actually controls are the optical stack, frame and thermal path, driver, dimming interface, finish, and recessed, surface, or suspended mounting hardware.

Use the [LED lighting manufacturer guide](/insights/led-lighting-manufacturer-guide) for factory capability, and the [office lighting design guide](/insights/office-lighting-design-guide) for layout and workplace criteria. Families below are square and round surface panels and recessed or grid modules, with linear and downlight complements.

## Which types of LED panel lights are there?

Buyers usually shortlist three panel formats, then decide whether linear runs or downlights should complete the ceiling. The right choice depends on the ceiling system, the room shape, and how even the emitting face needs to look in daily use.

- **Square backlit surface panels** for offices, meeting rooms, and retrofit ceilings — see the [square backlit LED surface panel](/products/square-backlit-led-surface-panel).
- **Ultra-slim round surface panels** for corridors, compact rooms, or circular layouts — see the [ultra-slim round surface panel light](/products/ultra-slim-round-surface-panel-light).
- **Recessed or grid-installed panels**, including troffer-style replacements, for standard ceiling modules. Confirm module size and grid fit.

Adjacent tools, not the focus here, include linear fixtures such as the [linear bar office suspended light](/products/linear-bar-office-suspended-light) — see the [LED linear light guide](/insights/led-linear-light-guide) — and recessed downlights such as the [classic recessed general downlight](/products/classic-recessed-general-downlight). A buyer who also needs a led downlight manufacturer should treat that as a separate category. The [linear LED batten light linkable](/products/linear-led-batten-light-linkable) can serve utility rooms, but is not a panel replacement.

| Panel type | Typical use | Key specs to define | Common pitfall |
|---|---|---|---|
| Square backlit surface | Offices, meeting rooms, retrofit ceilings | Uniformity, UGR target, CCT/CRI, driver and dimming, surface finish | Visible bright spots or a frame that does not sit flush |
| Ultra-slim round surface | Corridors, compact rooms, circular ceiling layouts | Diameter and depth, glare control, CCT match to nearby squares, mounting | Mixing round and square panels without a CCT/finish plan |
| Recessed / grid module | Standard ceiling grids and troffer replacements | Module size, cut-out or grid fit, UGR, access for maintenance, driver location | Ordering a module that does not fit the existing ceiling system |
| Linear / downlight complement | Open offices, circulation, accent layers | When to use linear runs or downlights instead of a full panel grid | Using panels alone where layers or accents are also required |

## Which specs decide LED panel light performance?

Panel performance is decided by how even the face looks, how the installation controls glare, and whether CCT, CRI, and dimming stay consistent across the ceiling. Lock those targets on the RFQ, then judge a lit sample with the photometric file.

**Uniformity and optical stack.** Backlit and edge-lit constructions change how even the face looks. Ask for a lit sample, not only a catalog photo.

**Glare / UGR.** Indoor panels are often specified against a workplace glare target. For ugr office lighting method detail, see [UGR in office lighting](/insights/ugr-office-lighting-explained). Do not invent a numeric rating; check the result against project criteria.

**CCT.** Keep color temperature consistent across a ceiling and adjacent fixtures. See the [color temperature guide](/insights/color-temperature-guide) and [3000K vs 4000K LED](/insights/3000k-vs-4000k-led).

**CRI.** Color rendering matters where finishes and skin tones are judged. If the project sets a cri 90 led requirement, put it on the schedule and ask for a matching report. See [what is CRI in lighting](/insights/what-is-cri-in-lighting).

**Driver, dimming, and mounting.** State 0–10 V, DALI, or another protocol on the RFQ, and confirm compatibility with the [LED dimming guide](/insights/dimming-guide). Recessed, surface, or suspended mounting and driver access belong in drawings.

Some projects also ask about tunable scenes; see the [human centric lighting guide](/insights/human-centric-lighting-guide). For light beyond a simple ceiling grid, use the [architectural LED lighting guide](/insights/architectural-led-lighting-guide).

## Which compliance requirements should buyers ask about?

Ask for market requirements you can verify against the exact SKU — never for vague catalog certificates or claims about what any one factory already holds. Safety reports, photometric files, and listing evidence should match the model you will order.

**Safety.** Consult [IEC 60598-1](https://webstore.iec.ch/en/publication/66620) as a general luminaire safety reference, and [UL 1598](https://www.shopulstandards.com/ProductDetail.aspx?productId=UL1598) for North America. Confirm accepted reports for the destination market.

**Workplace lighting context.** For offices and similar indoor workplaces, consult [ISO/CIE 8995-1](https://cie.co.at/publications/lighting-work-places-part-1-indoor) on the lighting of indoor work places. Use the UGR article linked above for method detail.

**Energy and rebate listings.** When the project needs them, check [DLC](https://www.designlights.org/). If ENERGY STAR comes up, use the [ENERGY STAR light fixtures](https://www.energystar.gov/products/light_fixtures) page; it no longer labels most fixture categories, so verify the exact model.

**Photometry and maintenance data.** Request IES files that reflect LM-79-style luminaire photometry for the quoted SKU and, where relevant, LM-80 and TM-21 source data matching the LED package in production. Do not invent numeric claims.

**Quality process.** Ask how [incoming quality control](/quality/iqc), [in-process quality control](/quality/ipqc), and [outgoing quality control](/quality/oqc) are run.

## How do you vet an LED panel light manufacturer?

Vetting an LED panel light manufacturer is a document-and-sample process, not a catalog comparison. Confirm that the factory can support the panel types on your plan, then ask for photometric files, a sample loop, and a quality process you can inspect.

- Category fit across panel types, not a one-SKU list
- IES photometric files for every model on the schedule
- A sample-and-approval loop for uniformity, CCT and CRI appearance, dimming, and finish
- Batch-to-batch CCT and finish consistency
- Documented incoming, in-process, and outgoing quality checks
- OEM or ODM capability when frames, sizes, or controls must be customized

An oem led lighting manufacturer relationship is useful for custom frames, sizes, finishes, or labeling; see the [OEM vs ODM lighting guide](/insights/oem-vs-odm-lighting-guide) and [OEM/ODM lighting services](/oem-odm). When the site is in China, treat the search as an led lighting manufacturer china problem and confirm who owns tooling, bins, and test reports. A named china lighting factory should match the quotation files; see [factory-first manufacturing](/factory-first), [how to source LED lights from China](/insights/how-to-source-led-lights-from-china), and [China lighting sourcing](/sourcing).

A qualified LED panel light manufacturer can hold those points without inventing certificates, minimum orders, lead times, or prices. For a general specification checklist, use the [commercial LED lighting guide](/insights/commercial-led-lighting-guide).

## What should a panel light RFQ include?

A panel light RFQ should give every bidder the same scope and evidence list, so quotes can be compared on equivalent terms. Include the ceiling type, fixture mix, visual-comfort method, color and control targets, and the documents, samples, and inspection steps required.

1. **Application and ceiling type,** including the existing or planned ceiling system.
2. **Fixture type per area:** square surface, round surface, or recessed/grid modules, plus linear or downlight complements.
3. **Visual comfort:** the glare method (UGR or the project equivalent), without inventing a numeric rating.
4. **CCT and CRI,** including whether adjacent families must match.
5. **Driver and control protocol:** 0–10 V, DALI, or another protocol in writing.
6. **Mounting details:** recessed, surface, or suspended; cut-out or grid dimensions; driver access.
7. **Finish:** frame color, surface treatment, and labeling.
8. **Destination-market evidence:** safety reports and rebate or listing documents with the quote.
9. **Samples and photometry:** a lit-sample plan and IES files for every SKU.
10. **Packing, inspection, and Incoterms.**

## FAQ

### What does an LED panel light manufacturer produce?

An LED panel light manufacturer builds slim indoor ceiling luminaires that deliver even general lighting for offices, classrooms, and similar commercial interiors. The factory controls the optical stack, frame, driver, dimming interface, finish, and mounting hardware — not just the LED board inside the panel.

### How do LED panel lights differ from linear lights or downlights?

Panels are typically used for wide, even ceiling illumination. Linear fixtures suit continuous runs and open-plan layouts, and downlights handle accents or circulation. Many projects combine them; choose the mix from the ceiling type and visual-comfort target, not from a single catalog page.

### Why does UGR matter when specifying LED panel lights?

UGR describes how glaring a lighting installation may feel in an indoor workplace. For offices and similar rooms, buyers should set a glare method and review a lit sample plus the model's photometric file, then check the result against the project's workplace criteria.

### Which compliance documents should I ask a panel light manufacturer for?

Ask for safety reports against IEC 60598 or UL/ETL listings for your market, photometric files (LM-79-style) and matching source-maintenance data, DLC listing status if you sell into US utility programs, and a written rule for substitutions. Verify each document against the exact SKU.

### How do I vet an LED panel light manufacturer before ordering?

Check category fit across panel types, request photometric files, run a structured sample program for uniformity, CCT/CRI, dimming, and finish, confirm batch consistency, review IQC/IPQC/OQC documentation, and confirm OEM or ODM support if you need customized frames, sizes, or controls.
