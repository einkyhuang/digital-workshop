A factory audit for lighting in China is a structured review of the production site, quality process, and records behind a supplier — not a showroom tour or a certificate file alone. Buyers should confirm the legal entity and production address, review the document pack, and inspect the line for their product family before treating a quotation as factory-direct.

![Empty commercial LED lighting factory assembly and aging area with fixtures on benches and racks, prepared for a factory audit, with no people or text visible.](/insights%20image/insights%20feature%20images/factory-audit-lighting-china-assembly-floor.webp)

## What is a factory audit for lighting in China?

A lighting factory audit reviews the actual production site, quality process, and supporting records before a first order or after a significant site or process change. It should connect the supplier's statements to observable evidence, not substitute a showroom presentation or certificate folder for production verification.

Turn a “factory audit lighting china” request into a defined scope: which product family, which address, and which records will be examined? Attach the review to the real China lighting factory, not a trading office with a convenient meeting room.

Choosing a manufacturer is a different task; see the [LED lighting manufacturer guide](/insights/led-lighting-manufacturer-guide). The [how to source LED lights from China](/insights/how-to-source-led-lights-from-china) article and [China lighting sourcing](/sourcing) page cover the broader purchasing path; this checklist concerns the audit and its deliverables only.

## Which types of lighting factory audits should buyers use?

Choose the audit type from the decision you need to make. A quality and capability review asks whether the site can build, inspect, and test your product family. A social-compliance review addresses a buyer's labor requirements. Neither scope should silently stand in for the other.

**Quality and capability:** examine the relevant line, aging and test areas, traceability, and inspection records. Use [incoming quality control](/quality/iqc), [in-process quality control](/quality/ipqc), and [outgoing quality control](/quality/oqc) for explanations of those gates rather than repeating them here.

**Social and labor compliance:** a buyer or brand may require [amfori BSCI](https://www.amfori.org/amfori-bsci/), [Sedex SMETA](https://www.sedex.com/solutions/smeta-audit/), or SA8000. Refer to the applicable program's requirements and agree the review scope. These are not claims about Formore's participation or results, and the programs should not be described as interchangeable certifications.

**Who conducts it:** a second-party audit is conducted by the buyer or its representative; a third-party audit involves an appointed audit firm. A lighting sourcing agent in China may arrange or accompany the visit, but is not the factory of record. Record responsibilities and any access restrictions before appointment.

## What should you review before visiting the factory?

Review a document pack before traveling so the visit tests evidence rather than collects brochures. Identify the legal entity, production address, product family, certificate scope, and process boundaries. Ask for explanations where names or locations differ, and use those explanations to plan which records and operations need checking.

Reconcile the quotation entity, factory of record, and visited site. Different names are not automatically misconduct: an exporter, manufacturer, and testing laboratory can have distinct roles. The relationships must be explained and traceable.

Refer to the [ISO 9001 family overview](https://www.iso.org/standards/popular/iso-9000-family): quality-management certification is not product safety approval or a substitute for an on-site audit. Check issuer, scope, site, and validity; consult [IAF](https://iaf.nu/en/home/) for accreditation context and verify the certification body's relevant accreditation rather than assuming a logo proves it.

Request destination-market files tied to the intended SKU, using [IEC 60598-1](https://webstore.iec.ch/en/publication/66620) as a luminaire-safety reference where applicable. The manufacturer guide linked above covers the broader document list.

Obtain a process map identifying which SMT, assembly, aging, photometric testing, and packing steps occur on site or are subcontracted. A website's [about Formore](/about) page or a [navigating Shenzhen](/insights/navigating-shenzhen) guide is background, not proof about the plant visited.

## What should an on-site lighting factory audit check?

Walk the line intended for your product family, then connect observations to work orders, samples, and inspection records. The aim is to understand what happens at this site and what remains unverified. Equipment in the room, without evidence of its use, does not establish a working quality process.

For a “factory audit lighting china” assignment, follow a sample or work order through the actual production and test route. Ask staff to show the associated material and inspection records, not just a finished fixture.

| Check | Evidence to examine | Question to record |
|---|---|---|
| Production route | Relevant line and work order | Is this where the proposed family will be built? |
| Quality gates | Incoming, in-process, and outgoing records | Can sampled checks be traced to the order? |
| Testing | Safety and photometric setup, equipment records | What was demonstrated, and what was only described? |
| Aging and packing | Batch records and packing instructions | Do controls match the intended construction? |
| Subcontracting | Process map and supplier-control records | Who remains accountable for off-site work? |

Where relevant, observe electrical-safety testing and request photometric measurement evidence from the integrating-sphere or goniophotometer setup. Record whether the demonstrated method and fixture match the intended product; do not invent acceptance values.

## What belongs in a useful factory audit report?

A useful report makes the review reconstructable: another buyer should understand which site was visited, what was examined, and what remains unresolved. Separate observed evidence from supplier statements and inaccessible records. A pass stamp without scope, site, and sampled evidence cannot support a defensible purchasing decision.

Make the “factory audit lighting china” report an evidence record, not a supplier brochure. It should include:

1. **Audit date and scope:** attendance, access restrictions, and agreed exclusions.
2. **Identity:** legal entity, production address, and explained relationships to the quotation party.
3. **Product family:** the proposed fixtures and the work orders or samples examined.
4. **Process boundaries:** operations observed in-house and those subcontracted.
5. **Equipment and gates:** test activities demonstrated, inspection stages visited, and supporting records sampled.
6. **Document register:** certificate, test-report, and inspection-record references, including site, scope, SKU, and validity limits.
7. **Gaps:** missing evidence and unresolved questions, distinguished from confirmed discrepancies.
8. **Follow-up:** action, responsible owner, agreed closure evidence, and how closure will be reviewed.

Do not claim that a gap is closed merely because a supplier promises a change. Keep the original finding and the supporting closure record linked.

Remote or video reviews can screen suppliers and support record checks, but are not equivalent to an on-site walk through production and test areas. Identify what could not be independently observed; do not erase that limitation from the final conclusion.

## How should audit findings shape the first order?

Use findings to lock the factory of record, production site, inspection plan, and treatment of open corrective actions before deposit or production release. The report should inform a documented buying decision, not become a blanket warranty. Identify conditions for proceeding and who will verify any promised corrective work.

## FAQ

### What is a factory audit for lighting in China?

A factory audit for lighting in China is a structured review of the production site, quality process, and records behind a supplier — not a showroom tour or a certificate check. Buyers use it to confirm the legal entity, production address, process capability, and quality evidence before a first order.

### Does an ISO 9001 certificate replace a factory audit?

No. An ISO 9001 certificate shows a quality-management system was certified for a stated scope and site at a point in time. Buyers should still verify the issuer, scope, site, and validity, then check whether the production line for their product family actually runs at that site.

### What should a lighting factory audit report include?

A useful report names the legal entity and production address, audit date and scope, product family reviewed, and processes seen in-house versus subcontracted. It should reference the documents sampled, record gaps, and list follow-up actions before the first order — not just a pass or fail stamp.

### Can a remote or video review replace an on-site factory audit?

A remote or video review can help screen a supplier and check records, but it is not equivalent to walking the production and test areas for the product family you will buy. Use it as a supplement, then confirm critical processes, equipment, and traceability on site before a first order.

### When should B2B buyers audit a lighting factory in China?

Audit before a first production order, and again when the product family, production site, or key process changes. A factory audit is especially useful when the quotation looks factory-direct, when an agent is involved, or when destination-market safety files need to be tied to the actual manufacturing site.
