**Subject:** Re: Little Swell – custom-printed, polyester-free kids' swimwear

Hi Abigail,

Thank you for reaching out and for sharing the vision behind Little Swell. A polyester-free kids' collection with your own original prints is a great direction, and we'd love to help bring it to life. Here are answers to your questions:

**1. Soft nylon/spandex with custom prints (rash guards and one-pieces)**
Yes. We recommend a recycled nylon/spandex jersey, around 78–82% recycled nylon and 18–22% spandex, at 180–220 gsm. It's soft, has good stretch and recovery, and works well for kids' rash guards and one-pieces. We'd use chlorine-resistant spandex so the garments keep their shape after repeated pool use.

For your original artwork we'd use acid digital printing, which is the right method for nylon (sublimation only works on polyester). It gives vivid colors, good resistance to chlorine and salt water, and needs no screens, so it suits a first collection with several prints.

**2. Polyester-free fabric options for printed board shorts**
For a first collection we recommend a recycled nylon/spandex stretch fabric (knit or woven, to be confirmed with fabric swatches), printed with the same artwork as your swimwear. This keeps the collection consistent and lets the prints share fabric minimums. Nylon Taslan, which has the classic board-short feel, is also possible, but its printing minimums are much higher, so it suits a later, larger run better.

**3. Printing partners**
[确认：我方是否自印锦纶] We work with experienced digital printing mills in Guangdong that specialize in printing on nylon/spandex, and we produce the garments from their printed fabric. You'll have one point of contact with us for the fabric, the printing and the garment production.

**4. Sampling process and timeline**
- Artwork review, fabric selection and quotation: about 1 week
- Print strike-offs and first fit samples (made in parallel): 7–10 days
- Shipping to Hawaii and your feedback: about 10 days
- Approved print samples: 10–14 days (our quote includes 2 rounds of color strike-offs)
- Pre-production samples plus third-party testing: about 2 weeks
- Bulk production after PP sample approval: about 6–8 weeks

In total, from receiving your artwork to delivery in Hawaii is roughly 4–4.5 months by sea or 3.5–4 months by air. If you're aiming for a summer 2027 launch, we'd suggest sending the artwork by the end of this year or early January.

**6. Children's product safety (US)**
Kids' swimwear sold in the US needs CPSIA third-party testing, a Children's Product Certificate (CPC) and tracking labels. We can also test for UPF, colorfastness and chlorine resistance.
